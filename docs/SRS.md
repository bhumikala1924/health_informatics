# Software Requirements Specification

## Introduction

### Purpose
The purpose of the Maternal and Child Health Tracking System is to provide a digital platform for managing maternal and child healthcare information.

## Functional Requirements

- Mother Registration
- Child Registration
- ANC Tracking
- Delivery Management
- PNC Monitoring
- Vaccination Tracking
- SMS Notifications

## Non-functional Requirements

- Performance
- Security
- Reliability
- Usability
